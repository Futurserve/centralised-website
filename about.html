About CBS

Local knowledge, backed by central capability.

Centralised Business Solutions exists to help independent property professionals and established property businesses operate more effectively.

{{ site.tagline }}

Relationships are the asset. Administration shouldn't hold them back.

We believe local relationships are one of the most valuable assets in property management, and they shouldn't be held back by the administrative burden of running a business. Our model combines local knowledge and relationships with central administrative capability, established processes and proven systems.

CBS is part of a group of property companies with long-established estate agency, rental management, scheme management and short-term rental operations, run from a central office.

We work with operators to find where support makes a genuine difference: taking on selected administrative functions, supporting an existing rental or scheme operation, or exploring the acquisition of an established business.
