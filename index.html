How we work

We take care of the back office. You take care of the business.

Property management is much more than looking after properties. Rent administration, financial records, owner statements, levy accounts, arrears, correspondence and reporting all take time, systems and specialist skills.

Centralised Business Solutions (CBS) provides that support from a central office, so local operators can concentrate on their clients, properties and communities.

  • You keep your local relationships and responsibility for the work on the ground.
  • We provide agreed administrative, financial and systems support.
  • Together, we build a more efficient operation that can grow.
