Services

Rental book administration

  • Rental administration and account processing
  • Rent collection monitoring and arrears administration
  • Deposit and lease administration

Schemes

Sectional title and scheme management support

Running a scheme takes consistent administration, accurate financial records and reliable communication. The group already does this for 73 sectional title schemes with more than 3,000 units.

CBS can provide central administrative, financial and secretarial support, including:

  • Levy administration and account management
  • Levy collection monitoring and arrears follow-up
  • Budgets and financial reporting
  • Creditor and payment administration, on the scheme's instructions
  • Meeting preparation, notices and minutes
  • Trustee and owner correspondence
  • Scheme records and administrative compliance support

Local scheme managers and service providers stay responsible for on-site relationships, inspections, maintenance and the practical management of the properties. Who is responsible for what is set out clearly in each agreement.

The benefit: a professional administrative operation behind the people who manage the schemes locally.

Short-term rentals

Short-term rental management

Holiday and short-stay properties earn well when guests are looked after and owners can see exactly what they've earned. The group already runs a dedicated short-term rental management business, with 115 holiday and short-stay properties under management.

  • Listings and booking management across the major platforms
  • Guest communication before, during and after each stay
  • Coordination of cleaning, changeovers and maintenance through local teams
  • Owner statements and income reporting

The benefit: owners and local operators get a professionally run short-term rental operation without building one themselves.
