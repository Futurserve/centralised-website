Partner with CBS

Independent locally. Supported nationally.

You don't need to be part of a large agency to benefit from the capability of a larger operation. CBS works with established independent property professionals and smaller agencies who want to build sustainable businesses, expand their services and increase their capacity.

For agents and agencies

Operate with the support of an established, compliant group.

Running a property business means juggling clients, transactions, administration, compliance, finances and business development. The right support structure makes a real difference.

You stay close to your clients and your local market. We provide the agreed infrastructure behind the operation.

Depending on your business, a partnership with CBS can include:

  • Rental and scheme administration handled centrally, so you can take on more without hiring.
  • Day-to-day admin support: listing uploads, offer-to-purchase assistance, deal tracking and a library of legal documents, with contract guidance from an experienced principal.
  • Marketing support: professional templates, design help, social media set-up and advertising on the major property portals.
  • New income streams: ongoing income from rentals you manage, and rewards for introducing schemes and other opportunities to the group's specialist teams.
  • Training and compliance: induction and legal training, system training, and help with qualifications and your Fidelity Fund Certificate.
  • Established processes and systems, without the cost of setting them up yourself.

Businesses we've bought are now running smoothly inside the group. Thinking about retirement, a new direction, or just wanting it off your plate? Let's talk. You don't need to have decided anything first.
